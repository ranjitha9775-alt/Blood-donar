Why donate?

A little of your time can mean everything to someone.

Safe blood donations help hospitals care for patients after accidents and surgery, during childbirth, and through treatments for serious illnesses. Blood cannot be manufactured; a safe supply depends on voluntary donors.

✓Donate through an authorised centre
✓Follow the clinician's screening
✓Share accurate health information
✓Ask professionals about eligibility

Eligibility and donation intervals depend on local guidance and individual health. A qualified healthcare professional makes the final assessment.
