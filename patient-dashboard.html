Find blood · Live donor search

Live matches include only registered donors who are confirmed available and separately opted in to show their name and area. Phone numbers, email addresses, and exact addresses are not shown in search; a donor's phone is shared only after they accept a specific request and consent to contact sharing. Location is approximate because the search does not collect coordinates.

Without a reliable search-origin coordinate, the 5/10/25/50 km choices return approximate city-level matches rather than calculated distances. Wider search checks opted-in donors across Karnataka. A required date matches a donor's saved date or current general confirmation. Exact blood-group matches are not medical compatibility advice; the treating blood bank must confirm compatibility.
